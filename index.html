Hi there, I am Ram Biradar

A product designer with a strategic mindset, based out of India 🇮🇳

Little bit about me

Hi there! If you're reading this, you probably want to know what drives my work. To me, every element of a design should be intelligent and backed by clear logic — an imaginative rush that also makes sense.

I started my career as a Graphics Designer after completing my Bachelor's in Computer Application. A deep curiosity about design and a pull toward product leadership led me to transition into full-time design roles. That single shift has defined everything since: making technology more accessible and enjoyable for everyone.

My work is an extension of my identity — both my art and my discipline. I treat my team, clients, and users with the same sincerity I bring to every pixel and decision.

My experience

With over 10+ years of experience in creating design solutions for web, mobile and desktop apps, and B2B SaaS based products, I excel at the intersection of strategy and execution. My core strengths lie in Product/UX Design and Product Management, where I translate complex user needs and business goals into intuitive, impactful solutions.

I am skilled in all aspects of the design process, from foundational Research & Data Analysis that drives key decisions to structuring the digital experience through robust Information Architecture. This is complemented by my expertise in creating and maintaining Design Systems & Styleguides, ensuring consistency, scalability, and efficiency across products.

Beyond the technical skill set, my leadership abilities are defined by fostering strong Cross-team Collaboration, acting as a bridge between design, engineering, and product teams.

I am committed to Team Motivation and Guidance, empowering designers to grow, innovate, and deliver their best work, ultimately leading to products that not only meet user expectations but also drive significant business growth.
